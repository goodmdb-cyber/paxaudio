Roger-300 FAQ

What is the output of the Roger-300?

The Roger-300 is a 300 W acoustic hailing device with a maximum SPL of 134 dB and a frequency response of 200 Hz – 8 kHz.

How big is the Roger-300?

The Roger-300 measures 29.6 x 18.7 x 27.5 cm without the bracket and weighs 9.4 kg.

Roger-300 or Roger-200?

The Roger-300 gives more output (300 W, 134 dB). The Roger-200 is lighter (6.2 kg, 200 W, 126 dB) and can change quickly from vehicle to handheld use. The Roger-200 is based on the technology of the Roger-300.
