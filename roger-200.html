Roger-200 FAQ

Is the Roger-200 suitable for vehicle mounting?

Yes. The Roger-200 is specifically designed for vehicle mounting, with a low-profile exterior that minimizes wind resistance. A handle attachment allows quick change from vehicle to handheld use.

How heavy is the Roger-200?

The Roger-200 weighs 6.2 kg and measures 170 x 170 x 350 mm.

Can the Roger-200 play wildlife deterrent sounds?

Yes. Its wideband frequency response (200 Hz – 8 kHz) supports realistic natural deterrent sounds as well as clear voice commands, for wildlife management and roadkill prevention.

What is supplied with the Roger-200?

Only the acoustic unit (speaker section) is supplied.
