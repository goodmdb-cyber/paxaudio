Compression Driver Units FAQ

Which compression driver units does PaxAudio offer?

300 W 8 ohm, 100 W 8 ohm, 100 W 11 ohm, 80 W 8 ohm and 75 W 8 ohm models, with SPL from 108 dB to 126 dB.

Which magnet types are used?

The 300 W, 100 W (8 ohm) and 80 W models use neodymium magnets. The 75 W model uses a ferrite magnet.
