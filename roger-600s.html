Roger-600S Long Range Acoustic Device

The Roger-600S is a long range acoustic device (acoustic hailing device) for notification up to 1 km. Its focused ±15° beam reduces the risk of audio overexposure to nearby personnel and minimizes distortion from reflected sounds. Designed and manufactured in Korea by PaxAudio.

Roger-600S FAQ

How far can the Roger-600S be heard?

The Roger-600S provides long range notification up to 1 km, with a maximum SPL of 135 dB at 1 m and a ±15° acoustic beam at 1 kHz.

What is supplied with the Roger-600S?

Only the acoustic unit (speaker section) is supplied. The acoustic unit measures 38 (W) x 48 (H) x 70 (D) cm and weighs 25 kg.

What is the difference between the Roger-600S and Roger-600R?

Both have the same acoustic performance: 135 dB @ 1 m, 250 Hz – 4 kHz and a ±15° beam. The Roger-600S acoustic unit is 38 x 48 x 70 cm and 25 kg. The Roger-600R is 31.5 x 31.5 x 75.3 cm and 23 kg.

Can the Roger-600S be used outdoors?

Yes. The Roger-600S has a waterproof design and supports handheld or self-standing operation.
