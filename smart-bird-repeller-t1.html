Smart Bird Repeller T1 FAQ

How does the T1 stop birds from getting used to it?

The T1 uses sound, light and glare at the same time: 360° predator calls, a rotating laser and a multi-angle reflective surface, so birds do not get used to a single stimulus.

Where can the T1 be used?

Orchards, rice fields and fish farms; airports, harbors and public works to prevent bird strikes; and buildings, parks and public spaces.

Is the T1 patented?

Yes. Patent No. 10-2941143, and it won the 2026 Korea Excellent Patent Award.
