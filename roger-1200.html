Roger-1200 Long Range Acoustic Device

The Roger-1200 is PaxAudio's flagship long range acoustic device (LRAD), also called an acoustic hailing device (AHD). It delivers clear voice messages up to 1 km and recorded warning signals up to 2 km, with a focused ±15° acoustic beam. Designed and manufactured in Korea, and built to each customer's requirements.

Roger-1200 FAQ

How far can the Roger-1200 be heard?

The Roger-1200 delivers a clear voice message up to 1 km, with a voice level of around 80 dB at 1 km. With a recorded signal, long range notification reaches up to 2 km. The sound impact range is up to 100 m.

What is included with the Roger-1200?

The Roger-1200 is composed of the acoustic unit and an amplifier. The acoustic unit weighs 20 kg without the stand and cradle.

What power supply does the Roger-1200 need?

The Roger-1200 runs on 100 - 240 VAC power input.

Who uses the Roger-1200?

Long range acoustic devices such as the Roger-1200 are used by law enforcement, border security, defense and maritime operators for warnings over distance, and to keep wildlife away from airport runways and agricultural areas.

How is the Roger-1200 different from other Roger models?

The Roger-1200 is the flagship of the Roger series, with the highest sound power (151 dB) and the longest range. The Roger-600S and 600R reach 135 dB and notify up to 1 km. The Roger-300, 200 and 100 are compact 300 W, 200 W and 100 W models for vehicles, handheld use and PTZ camera systems.
