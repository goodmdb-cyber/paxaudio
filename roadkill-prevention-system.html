Detect, deter and warn

Features

  • Motion / thermal imaging sensor detects wildlife within a 20-meter radius
  • LED flasher blinks automatically on detection
  • Speaker emits multiple frequency bands simultaneously to deter animals
  • LED warning light flashes to alert drivers to slow down
  • Functions stay active for about one minute after activation

How it works

  1. Install the devices at roadkill hotspot areas
  2. Approaching wildlife is detected by the motion / thermal imaging sensor
  3. The LED flash and specific sounds warn and repel the wildlife
  4. The LED warning light turns on to alert drivers to slow down

Roadkill Prevention System FAQ

How does the roadkill prevention system detect animals?

A motion / thermal imaging sensor detects wildlife within a 20-meter radius.

What happens when an animal is detected?

An LED flasher and a multi-band speaker deter the animal, and an LED warning light alerts drivers to slow down. Both stay active for about one minute.
