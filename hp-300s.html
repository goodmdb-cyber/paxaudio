HP-300S High-Power Ultrasonic Wildlife Repellent Speaker

Designed for vehicles, trains and specialized transport units, the HP-300S 300 W high-frequency speaker deters large wild animals such as elephants and cattle from railway tracks and roadways, helping prevent delays and reduce the risk of serious accidents.

Clear the track before the train arrives

Real-world application

Mounted on the front upper section of trains, the system lets operators activate high-frequency sound bursts when approaching wildlife, minimizing stoppage time and improving safety where animals cross.

Optimized for large mammals

Particularly effective against large mammals, with a robust, high-output design for special-purpose vehicles in remote or high-risk zones.
