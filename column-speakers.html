Column Speakers (LA / LS series) FAQ

What is the difference between the LA and LS series?

The LA series (250 Hz – 15 kHz, 110 – 118 dB) uses 5" 10 W drivers for high-sensitivity voice paging. The LS series (60 Hz – 20 kHz) adds a tweeter for wider-range sound.

What material and colors are available?

Aluminum housing in silver and black.
